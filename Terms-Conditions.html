biotech 2. Scope of Services

AumNamah Radioanalytical Laboratory provides the following services in accordance with our NABL accreditation (TC-17551) and ISO/IEC 17025:2017 quality management system:

  • Radioanalytical Testing: Analysis of gross alpha/beta activity, gamma spectrometry, radon/thoron measurements, and specific radionuclide identification in various matrices.
  • Calibration Services: Calibration of radiation monitoring instruments and dosimeters.
  • Consultation: Expert consultation on radiation safety, environmental monitoring, and regulatory compliance.

The specific scope of each engagement is determined by the test request form submitted by the Client and confirmed by the Laboratory.
