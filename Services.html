Comprehensive quantification of naturally occurring and anthropogenic radionuclides across diverse matrices using state-of-the-art spectrometry and radiochemistry methodologies.

agriculture

Food & Agriculture

Comprehensive gamma spectrometry analysis to ensure the safety and compliance of food products, agricultural yields, and animal feeds against international radiological standards.

Analyzed Categories

  • Bakery & Confectionary: Breads, Cakes, Biscuits, Sweets
  • Crops & Produce: Cereals, Millets, Pulses, Fruits, Vegetables
  • Marine & Meats: Fishes, Crustaceans, Red Meats, Processed Meats
  • Milk & Poultry: Milk Powders, Cheese, Eggs, Feed Supplements
  • Spices & Others: Condiments, Honey, Beverages, Tea, Tobacco
Detecting Gamma Emitting Radionuclides: Cs-134, Cs-137, Co-60, K-40, Iodine-131, Sr-90
